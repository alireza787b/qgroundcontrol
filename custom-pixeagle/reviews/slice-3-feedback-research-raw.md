The smallest useful changes to put in front of the operator are:

1. Replace the three-line top card with a compact owner/status row near the tracking controls. In routine companion-only use, show the source identifier once; put “No aircraft” and the full connection explanation in details. With an associated vehicle, retain its short identity visibly, especially after a vehicle switch and in fullscreen. Use existing verified labels or the configured endpoint in details; do not invent a physical aircraft/companion identity.
2. Keep the tracking state and stop action explicit. Short states such as Ready, Acquiring, Tracking, Lost, Updating, and View only can reduce prose. “Stop tracking” is clearer for an active operation than “Cancel tracking.” Put connection/permission troubleshooting in expanded details, while leaving an actionable error or stale-state message visible until resolved.
3. Allow a permitted active PixEagle track to be replaced by clicking a point or dragging a box without first pressing Retarget. Keep the initial selection entry point discoverable and show the short gesture hint when it is useful. In Smart mode, expose the applicable detected-target click instruction instead of implying rectangle support. A direct gesture still belongs to one current companion, video surface, frame, and target context.
4. Preserve the current fullscreen double-click delay and cancellation. Quick retarget means one fewer deliberate action; it does not mean zero delay. Do not commit the first click of a double-click, reuse a pending click after an owner change, or fall through to native gimbal control. Give clear feedback if the original frame/context is no longer valid by dispatch time.
5. Change the setup affordance to the existing gear with an accessible “Tracking settings” name, retaining a discoverable label where needed for the feedback pass. Keep tracker/mode choices in that panel. Use QGC controls and palette, constrain the panel within available Fly View space, and preserve touch-sized targets.
6. Keep aircraft following outside these controls. If following is reported active, retain a clear blocking message and the existing route for stopping it. A shortened routine label must not silently remove that exceptional-state information.

A toolbar indicator with an expandable drawer is a sound later consolidation if the small local change still leaves too much overlay. QGC documents separate app and vehicle indicator extension points; app indicators can exist without a connected aircraft. The local `QGCCorePlugin::toolBarIndicators()` declaration is the exact API to inspect for this branch. A complete toolbar replacement would be disproportionate to this feedback pass. [QGC Toolbar Customization](https://docs.qgroundcontrol.com/master/en/qgc-dev-guide/custom_build/toolbar.html)
